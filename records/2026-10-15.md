### Current progress

Today was the student kick-off event. I started helping with setup at 9:00 a.m. The event ran from 11:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., and cleanup continued until about 2:00 p.m. The office finished early, so I did not work on other projects afterward.

Our team prepares posters and giveaways for this student event each semester. In the past, we made about 100 to 120 stickers, but more than two-thirds usually remained. I proposed making pins because I wanted students to have something meaningful from the event that they would want to keep.

We ordered 100 pins, and fewer than five were left after the event. I also heard positive comments about them several times during the event.

As a UX/UI student, this was a useful reminder to think about what people would actually want to take and keep, alongside how an item looks. I felt good seeing a positive response to a change I had suggested. Even in a campus job with an event that repeats every semester, there is room to question the usual approach and try something better.
